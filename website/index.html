A FEW CLEAR ANSWERS

Before you begin.

Can I open a real account?

Not yet. This is a design and product preview. It does not accept money or identity documents, or issue real cards.

Is USDT available?

It is planned but disabled. The operating arrangement, eligibility, partners and supported networks must be confirmed before launch.

Are prices and locations final?

No. Amounts and locations in the demo are illustrative. Actual fees and availability must be shown before each transaction is confirmed at launch.
